Obfuscated Natural Number Game Evaluates Architectural Reasoning in LLM Provers

Researchers identified Architectural Reasoning — the ability to synthesize formal proofs using exclusively local axioms in an alien math domain — as the necessary capacity for future automated theorem discovery AI. Using the Obfuscated Natural Number Game in Lean 4 (a zero-knowledge, closed environment created by renaming identifiers), they revealed a universal latency tax and a clean divergence in robustness.

Modular Forms and Numerical Explorations of Rational Approximations to ζ(3)

Researchers revisited Beukers' modular-form proof of the irrationality of ζ(3) — Apéry's constant — from the perspective of the auxiliary weight-two modular form for the Fricke group Γ₀(6)⋆. They showed Beukers' choice belongs to a one-parameter affine family, all achieving the same exponential decay as classical Apéry approximations. A rare instance of pure number theory in AI-adjacent research, accepted alongside AI reasoning papers at AAAI 2026 Spring Symposium.
