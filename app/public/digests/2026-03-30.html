OpenAI Kills Sora and Cancels $1B Disney Deal to Bet on Robotics and AGI

OpenAI abruptly announced it is discontinuing Sora — its AI video generation app, social network, and API — reallocating compute toward world simulation research to advance robotics, a move that also canceled a $1 billion equity deal with Disney signed just four months ago in December 2025. OpenAI had been releasing Sora updates as recently as this week before the shutdown, suggesting a rapid strategy pivot; the company cited growing compute demand, rising energy costs, and intent to build a consolidated 'super app' folding ChatGPT, Codex, and browser Atlas into one interface.
