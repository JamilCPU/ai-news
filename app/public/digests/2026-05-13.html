OpenAI shares insights from Parameter Golf research on model scaling

OpenAI published "What Parameter Golf taught us," sharing insights from a systematic exploration of how model parameters affect performance across different architectures and training regimes. The research revealed that parameter count alone is an incomplete predictor of capability, with factors like data quality, training duration, and architectural choices playing significant roles in determining model performance.

Key Findings
  • Parameter count is an incomplete predictor of model capability — the headline metric routinely cited in announcements
  • Data quality and training duration play equally or more significant roles in determining final performance
  • Architectural choices across different training regimes compound the effect, making comparisons by size alone misleading
  • More nuanced approaches to model design and training are needed to achieve optimal performance at any given scale
