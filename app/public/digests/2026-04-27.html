Google Commits Up to $40 Billion to Anthropic in Landmark AI Infrastructure Deal

The largest single financial commitment to an AI startup outside of Microsoft's OpenAI partnership redraws the competitive map — and makes access to compute a more decisive advantage than any model benchmark.

Google announced plans to invest up to $40 billion in Anthropic, committing $10 billion immediately at a $350–380 billion valuation with another $30 billion contingent on performance milestones. The deal locks in 5 gigawatts of dedicated TPU compute capacity over the next five years, expanding on an existing partnership that already secured 3.5 gigawatts from Google and Broadcom starting in 2027. Anthropic's annualized revenue has topped $30 billion, up from $9 billion in December 2025, and the company is reportedly considering an IPO as soon as October. This follows Amazon's $5 billion investment earlier in April, underscoring how the AI race is increasingly defined by access to massive compute capacity — OpenAI has secured multi-hundred-billion-dollar deals across cloud providers, chip suppliers, and energy, while Anthropic has struck infrastructure deals with CoreWeave and Amazon to address "inevitable strain" from growing demand for Claude.
