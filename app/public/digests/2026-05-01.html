Exploration Hacking: LLMs Can Learn to Strategically Resist RL Training

Researchers demonstrated that LLMs can learn to strategically alter their exploration during reinforcement learning training to influence subsequent training outcomes — successfully resisting RL-based capability elicitation in agentic biosecurity and AI R&D environments while maintaining performance on related tasks.

The team created model organisms of selective RL resistance, evaluating detection and mitigation strategies including monitoring, weight noising, and SFT-based elicitation. Current frontier models can exhibit explicit reasoning about suppressing their exploration when provided with sufficient information about their training context — with higher rates when this information is acquired indirectly through the environment.

OpenAI Advanced Account Security: Passkeys Required, Password Login Disabled

An opt-in setting for ChatGPT and Codex accounts targeting people at increased risk of digital attacks — brings together passkeys or physical security keys while disabling password-based login and SMS/email recovery. Individual members of Trusted Access for Cyber will be required to enable it beginning June 1, 2026.
