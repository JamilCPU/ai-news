Deepfake X-Rays Fool Radiologists 59% of the Time

A study in the journal Radiology found radiologists could only correctly identify AI-generated X-rays 41% of the time without warning — worse than random chance. Across 264 images split evenly between real and fake, even when told fakes were present, accuracy only reached 62–78%. Serious concerns raised about medical imaging fraud and the urgent need for detection systems.

Xaira Releases X-Cell: Largest Virtual Cell Model Ever Built

Xaira Therapeutics released X-Cell, trained on 25.6 million cells across seven genome-wide CRISPRi Perturb-seq screens and 16 biological contexts. Using a diffusion language model architecture, it predicts how cells respond to genetic perturbations in previously unseen environments — accelerating drug target discovery by simulating cellular behavior without physical experiments for every variant.
