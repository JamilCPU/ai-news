OpenAI Models, Codex, and Managed Agents Come to AWS in Limited Preview

This period's defining move: OpenAI and AWS expanded their strategic partnership to bring OpenAI capabilities to Amazon Bedrock in limited preview, giving enterprises access to frontier models, coding agents, and managed agents within their existing AWS infrastructure, security protocols, and compliance requirements.

The expanded partnership covers three pillars: OpenAI models on AWS (including GPT-5.5 on Amazon Bedrock), Codex on AWS (powering the coding harness with models served from Bedrock), and Amazon Bedrock Managed Agents powered by OpenAI. More than 4 million people now use Codex every week. The move follows OpenAI's recent amendment with Microsoft granting multi-cloud flexibility — positioning OpenAI to serve customers across all major cloud providers simultaneously.

RecursiveMAS: Scaling Agent Collaboration Through Recursion Achieves 8.3% Accuracy Improvement

A recursive multi-agent framework that casts the entire system as a unified latent-space recursive computation — extending the scaling principle of recursive language models from single models to multi-agent systems. Evaluated across 9 benchmarks spanning math, science, medicine, search, and code, RecursiveMAS delivered 8.3% average accuracy improvement, 1.2x–2.4x inference speedup, and 34.6–75.6% token usage reduction.

Semi-Markov RL for City-Scale EV Ride-Hailing Achieves $1.22M Profit with Zero Feeder-Limit Violations

A hex-grid semi-Markov decision process with mixed discrete/continuous actions for dispatch, repositioning, and charging of EV fleets — constrained by charger capacity and power grid feeder limits. Tested on a large-scale NYC taxi simulator, PD-RSAC achieved $1.22M net profit versus $0.58–0.70M for heuristic and RL baselines, while maintaining zero feeder-limit violations throughout.

Action-Aware Generative Sequence Modeling for Short Video Recommendation Boosts Retention by 0.162%

A2Gen refines user actions along the temporal dimension and chains them into sequences for unified prediction, using a hierarchical sequence encoder and an autoregressive generator. Deployed across all traffic on Kuaishou's platform serving 400 million daily users, the model improved watch time by 0.34%, interaction rate by 8.1%, and overall user retention (LifeTime-7) by 0.162%.

StratFormer: Adaptive Opponent Modeling and Exploitation in Imperfect-Information Games

A transformer-based meta-agent that learns to simultaneously model and exploit opponents through a two-phase curriculum — first learning behavioral patterns, then progressively shifting toward best-response exploitation. On Leduc Hold'em tested against six opponent archetypes, StratFormer achieved average exploitation gain of +0.106 BB per hand, with peak gains of +0.821 against highly exploitable opponents while maintaining near-equilibrium safety.

SkillSynth: Scalable Terminal Task Synthesis via Skill Graphs

An automated framework for terminal task synthesis built on a scenario-mediated skill graph, where scenarios serve as intermediate nodes connecting diverse command-line skills. Graph-sampled workflow paths are instantiated into executable tasks by a multi-agent harness. Task instances from SkillSynth were used to train Hy3 Preview, contributing to enhanced agentic capabilities in terminal-based settings.

OpenAI Publishes Detailed Commitment to Community Safety: Violence Prevention, Detection, and Law Enforcement Escalation

OpenAI outlined its formal framework for minimizing uses of its services in furtherance of violence or harm: models trained to refuse requests that could meaningfully enable violence, automated detection systems (classifiers, hash-matching, blocklists), human review for flagged conversations, and law enforcement escalation when conversations indicate imminent and credible risk to others.
