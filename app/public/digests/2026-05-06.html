Subquadratic Claims 1,000× AI Efficiency Gain with SubQ Model; Researchers Demand Independent Proof

Miami-based startup Subquadratic emerged from stealth claiming to have built the first large language model with fully subquadratic architecture, where compute grows linearly with context length instead of quadratically — a distinction that, if true, would represent a fundamental shift in the economics of long-context AI.

The company asserts its SubQ 1M-Preview model reduces attention compute by almost 1,000 times compared to frontier models at 12 million tokens, raised $29 million in seed funding at a $500 million valuation, and launched three products in private beta. The AI research community reacted with skepticism ranging from genuine curiosity to accusations of vaporware, noting parallels to Magic.dev's similar claims in 2024 that never materialized, and demanding independent verification of the extraordinary efficiency claims.

OpenAI Updates ChatGPT to GPT-5.5 Instant with Memory Sources for Transparency

OpenAI replaced GPT-5.3 Instant as the default ChatGPT model with GPT-5.5 Instant, which internal evaluations show produces 52.5% fewer hallucinated claims on high-stakes prompts covering medicine, law, and finance, and reduces inaccurate claims by 37.3% on challenging conversations. The company introduced memory sources across all ChatGPT models, giving users visibility into what context shaped personalized responses with controls to delete or correct outdated information, though the feature may not show every factor that influenced an answer. The new model delivers tighter, more concise answers with better use of past chats, files, and connected Gmail for personalization while maintaining warmth and personality.

Training Reasoning Planners with Executor-Grounded Rewards

A new method trains reasoning planners using executor-grounded rewards, where the reward signal comes from actual execution of planned actions rather than proxy metrics or human feedback. The approach addresses the challenge of training AI systems to plan effectively when the true quality of a plan can only be assessed by executing it and observing outcomes — enabling more reliable planning in domains like software development, robotics, and multi-step reasoning.

OpenAI Releases MRC Networking Protocol Through Open Compute Project

OpenAI released MRC (Multipath Reliable Connection), a novel protocol for GPU networking in large training clusters, through the Open Compute Project as an open standard. Developed in partnership with AMD, Broadcom, Intel, Microsoft, and NVIDIA over two years, MRC enables multi-plane high-speed networks for supercomputers with over 100,000 GPUs using only two tiers of switches, reduces core congestion through adaptive packet spraying, and uses SRv6 source routing to bypass failures in microseconds. The protocol is already deployed across OpenAI's largest NVIDIA GB200 supercomputers and has been used to train multiple frontier models.

OpenAI Rearchitected WebRTC Stack for Low-Latency Voice AI at Scale

OpenAI rearchitected its WebRTC stack to deliver low-latency voice AI at scale for more than 900 million weekly active users, using a split relay plus transceiver architecture that preserves standard WebRTC behavior while changing internal packet routing. The new design exposes a small, fixed UDP surface to the public internet, routes every packet to the transceiver that owns the corresponding WebRTC session using protocol-native routing hints, and deploys globally through geographically distributed relay ingress points to reduce first-hop latency.
