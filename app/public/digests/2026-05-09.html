Anthropic Introduces Dreaming: AI Agents That Learn From Their Own Mistakes

Anthropic's dreaming system lets AI agents learn from their own mistakes by simulating alternative actions and outcomes without actually executing them — exploring counterfactual scenarios, learning from failures, and improving decision-making over time without the cost or risk of real-world trial and error.

Alongside dreaming, Anthropic moved outcomes and multi-agent orchestration from research preview to public beta, making these capabilities more widely available to developers building agentic systems.
