Anthropic hits $30 billion revenue run rate after 80x growth

80× Revenue growth to reach $30B ARR · Code with Claude conference

Anthropic announced it hit a $30 billion revenue run rate after experiencing 80x growth, with CEO Dario Amodei revealing the milestone at the Code with Claude conference. The company's explosive growth underscores the rapid adoption of Claude across enterprise and consumer markets, positioning Anthropic as a major player in the AI industry alongside OpenAI and Google. This revenue milestone represents a significant achievement for a company that has focused on building safe, beneficial AI systems.

OpenAI launches Deployment Company to help businesses build around intelligence

OpenAI launched the OpenAI Deployment Company, a new entity designed to help businesses build and deploy AI systems, acquiring Tomoro, an applied AI consulting firm, and partnering with 19 leading global investment firms, consultancies, and system integrators. The company launches with more than $4 billion of initial investment, with partners including the world's top management consultancies and private equity firms. The Deployment Company will help enterprises integrate AI into their operations, from strategy and design through implementation and scaling.
