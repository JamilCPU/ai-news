AI-Hallucinated Citations Are Contaminating Peer-Reviewed Science at Scale

A Nature analysis found that tens of thousands of 2025 scientific publications may contain AI-hallucinated references — citations that appear plausible but point to papers that do not exist. Unlike ordinary citation errors, fabricated references are semantically coherent, making them invisible to conventional editorial screening and extremely difficult to retract at scale. Nature calls for mandatory AI disclosure in paper submissions, automated citation-validation tooling at journals, and systematic retraction pipelines.
