vLLM V0 to V1 Migration: Correctness Before Corrections in Reinforcement Learning

ServiceNow researchers documented a framework-level lesson from their migration: fixing inference backend correctness before addressing objective-side corrections eliminates train-inference mismatch in online RL systems. Four concrete issues required patching — processed rollout logprobs, V1-specific runtime defaults, inflight weight-update path, and fp32 lm_head. The lesson has broad applicability for any team running online RL pipelines.
